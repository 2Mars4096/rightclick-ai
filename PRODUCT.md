# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who work across writing, research, scheduling, and communication apps and want to act on selected text without breaking focus. They use direct Services for repeat actions and open the native workspace only when they need review, clipboard history, or more control.

## Product Purpose

RightClick AI makes selected-text intelligence feel like a dependable part of macOS. Install it once, invoke a precise action from the context menu, and get a useful result quickly. The product succeeds when routine actions stay out of the way, consequential actions remain reviewable, and the clipboard workspace is always ready as a fallback.

## Brand Personality

Ready, restrained, dependable. The product should feel like a cowboy's trusted sidearm: close at hand, quick to draw, powerful when needed, and never ornamental or fussy. Its voice is direct, calm, and specific.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a chat-first assistant, a crowded launcher, a generic SaaS dashboard, or a developer console presented as consumer UI. Avoid decorative glass, neon tool aesthetics, oversized cards, repeated instructions, competing primary actions, and technical detail in everyday paths.

## Design Principles

1. The shortest path stays outside the window. Direct Services are the primary repeat-use surface.
2. One obvious next action. Each state makes the next useful move visually unmistakable.
3. Power waits within reach. Advanced controls and technical detail are available progressively without crowding routine work.
4. Respond immediately. Long operations never freeze the interface and always show honest progress, completion, or recovery guidance.
5. Earn trust through native behavior. Familiar macOS controls, keyboard operation, local-first clipboard handling, and precise status feedback matter more than decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use the native macOS accessibility baseline: full keyboard operation, useful VoiceOver labels and values, visible focus and state feedback, sufficient contrast without relying on color alone, comfortable target sizes, Dynamic Type-compatible system typography where available, and no decorative motion that conflicts with reduced-motion preferences.
